FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Zitch?
Zitch is a Nigerian bill-payment and VTU (virtual top-up) platform. From one app you can buy airtime and data, print recharge PINs, renew cable TV, pay electricity and internet bills, buy exam PINs and fund betting wallets.
Which networks and billers are supported?
Airtime and data on MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile; DStv, GOtv and StarTimes for cable TV; all major electricity distribution companies; leading internet providers; WAEC, JAMB and NECO for exam PINs; and popular betting platforms.
How fast is delivery?
Airtime, data and recharge PINs are delivered instantly. Electricity tokens and cable activations typically arrive within seconds of a successful payment.
Is my money and data safe?
Every payment is protected by your transaction PIN, and all data moves over encrypted connections. Wallet funding is handled by trusted, licensed payment partners — we never store your card details.
What happens if a transaction fails?
Failed transactions are automatically reversed to your Zitch wallet, usually within minutes. If a reversal is ever delayed, our support team will resolve it for you.
How do I fund my wallet?
Fund instantly by bank transfer to your dedicated account number, by debit card, or via USSD — all through our secure payment partners.
