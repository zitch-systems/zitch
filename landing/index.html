Questions

Everything people ask, answered plainly.

The answers we would give you in a chat.

What exactly is Zitch?
Your bank app and your chat app, finally the same thing. Zitch holds your naira and your dollars, pays every bill and sends money to any Nigerian bank — from the app, or by texting us on WhatsApp.
What can I actually pay for?
If it takes your money every month, Zitch already handles it: airtime, data, electricity, DStv, GOtv and StarTimes, WAEC and JAMB pins, betting wallets, bank transfers and fixed savings.
How do I send money on WhatsApp?
Text it how you would say it. That is the whole flow.
  1. Reply 2 on the menu, or type "send 5k to Ada".
  2. Fill in the details on the private form.
  3. Zitch shows you the account name before anything moves.
  4. Confirm with your PIN.
  5. Your receipt arrives in the same chat.
How much does Zitch charge?
Zitch-to-Zitch is free and always will be. Bank transfers are ₦10. Airtime and data earn up to 3% back — and there is no card maintenance charge waiting at the end of the month.
Is banking by chat actually safe?
Zitch never acts on a message alone. Every naira needs your fingerprint, Face ID or 4-digit PIN, one wrong attempt cancels the whole flow, and we will never ask you for your PIN in a conversation.
Do I have to download the app?
No download. Chat banking runs on the WhatsApp already on your phone. The app is there when you want cards, savings and multi-currency — not as a toll gate in front of them.
How fast do transactions land?
Airtime in 3 seconds, transfers in under a minute. Tokens, pins and receipts come straight back into the same chat. And when a network is having a bad day, we tell you before you pay — not after.
What if a payment fails?
Failed means reversed, automatically. A failed bill or top-up returns to your wallet, usually in minutes and always within 24 hours. Every attempt stays in your history with a reference you can quote to support.
Can I hold dollars?
Naira, dollars, pounds and Canadian dollars sit on one balance screen. Convert at a rate you can see, locked for 60 seconds so nothing shifts while you decide. Then spend the dollars anywhere online with a Zitch virtual card.
Why do you need my BVN and NIN?
Because it is your money, and it should stay only yours. Verification is what stops someone else opening an account in your name. It takes about two minutes, and your higher limits unlock the moment it clears.
Why not just use my bank app?
Because your bank does not answer at 11pm on a Sunday. Zitch does: the same transfers and the same bills, reachable from the chat you are already in, with the rate and the fee printed on the receipt instead of buried in a statement.
