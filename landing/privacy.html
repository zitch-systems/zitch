01 Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Zitch mobile app, website and related services (the "Platform"), operated by Zitch Technologies Ltd (RC 0000000), 1 Example Close, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria ("Zitch", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act and applicable regulations. This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

02 Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your name, phone number, email address and password.
  • Identity information — where required to verify you or meet legal obligations, details such as date of birth or a government identifier.
  • Transaction details — the phone numbers, meter numbers, smartcard/IUC numbers, billers and amounts you use to make payments.
  • Support messages — anything you send us when you contact support.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage & device data — app/site interactions, device type, operating system, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Log & security data — records used to keep your account safe and detect fraud.

03 How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account and Wallet;
  • process your transactions and deliver the value you buy;
  • verify your identity and prevent fraud, money laundering and abuse;
  • provide customer support and resolve disputes and reversals;
  • send service messages such as receipts, alerts and security notices;
  • improve and secure the Platform; and
  • send promotional messages where you have agreed to receive them — you can opt out at any time.

04 Legal basis for processing

Depending on the activity, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (to deliver the Services), your consent (for example, marketing), legal obligations (such as anti-fraud and record-keeping duties), and our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Platform. You can withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on it.

05 When we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Platform:

  • Billers — the network, disco, pay-TV or exam body that must receive your details to fulfil a transaction (e.g. the phone or meter number).
  • Payment Partners — licensed processors that handle your wallet funding.
  • Service providers — vetted vendors (e.g. cloud hosting, communications, analytics) that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal & regulatory — authorities where we are required by law, or to prevent fraud or protect our rights and users.
  • Business transfers — a successor entity if Zitch is involved in a merger, acquisition or reorganisation, subject to this policy.

06 Payment information

Card and bank payments are processed by our licensed Payment Partners. Zitch does not store your full card number, CVV or bank credentials. Those details are handled directly by the payment processor under their own security standards.

We keep a record of your transactions (amounts, billers, references and status) so we can provide receipts, support and reversals, and to meet our legal obligations.

07 Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and for as long afterwards as we need to meet legal, accounting, tax and fraud-prevention requirements. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

08 How we protect your information

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption of data in transit, PIN-protected transactions, access controls and monitoring. No system is completely secure, so we also encourage you to protect your PIN and password and to tell us right away about anything suspicious.

09 Cookies & analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect how the site works.

10 Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete your information where there is no lawful reason to keep it;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent;
  • request a copy of certain information in a portable format; and
  • lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@zitch.ng. We may need to verify your identity before we act on a request.

11 Children

Zitch is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

12 International transfers

Some of our service providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we take steps to ensure your information receives a level of protection consistent with applicable data-protection law.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email. Please review it periodically.
