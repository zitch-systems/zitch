Our banking partner

Zitch, powered by Wema Bank

Every Zitch service, in the app and on WhatsApp, is provided by Zitch and runs on Wema Bank's banking infrastructure.

  • In partnership with Wema BankZitch works in partnership with Wema Bank Plc. Your Zitch account number is a Wema Bank account, and your money is held by Wema Bank.
  • Licensed by the Central Bank of NigeriaWema Bank Plc is a commercial bank licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).
  • Deposits insured by the NDICDeposits held with Wema Bank are insured by the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC), within the limits the NDIC sets.

Who does what

Zitch designs and runs the services you use: the Zitch app, Zitch on WhatsApp, transfers, airtime and data, bills and the rest. Wema Bank provides the banking infrastructure behind them: your account, the movement of your money, and the safekeeping of your deposits.

Using Zitch on WhatsApp

When you confirm a payment on WhatsApp, you enter your PIN in a secure WhatsApp form. The PIN is never sent to the chat. The payment is then processed on Wema Bank's infrastructure, and your receipt arrives in the chat.

Questions

Contact Zitch support at support@zitch.ng, or from WhatsApp reply menu and choose customer care.
